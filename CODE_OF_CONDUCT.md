# Code of Conduct

Project participants are expected to communicate professionally, focus criticism
on technical content, avoid harassment and discrimination, respect privacy, and
handle security disclosures responsibly.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, off-topic, deceptive, or unsafe content
and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its
contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. This concise
policy may be replaced by a foundation-standard code of conduct as governance
expands.
